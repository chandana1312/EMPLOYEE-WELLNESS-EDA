# Employee Wellness EDA

## Overview
This project explores employee mental health data to understand the factors related to mental health treatment.

## Objective
To identify patterns and factors associated with employees reporting mental health treatment.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Key Areas Analysed
- Treatment distribution
- Gender and treatment
- Family history and treatment
- Work interference and treatment
- Mental health benefits and treatment
- Multivariate relationships using correlation analysis

## Key Finding
Family history and work interference showed strong differences in treatment responses, while workplace mental health benefits were also associated with higher treatment responses.
