# 🧠 Employee Wellness & Mental Health Analysis

### What factors are linked to mental health treatment at work?

Mental health in the workplace can be difficult to see, but data can reveal patterns that aren't immediately obvious.

This project explores employee mental health data to understand **who reports receiving mental health treatment and which personal and workplace factors are related to it.**

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

To explore employee wellness data and identify patterns and factors associated with **mental health treatment**.

---

## 🔍 What We Explored

- 🧑‍💼 Employee demographics
- 🧠 Mental health treatment
- 👨‍👩‍👧 Family history
- 💼 Work interference
- 🏢 Workplace mental health benefits
- 📊 Relationships between multiple factors

---

## 🛠️ Analysis Process

**Raw Data → Data Cleaning → EDA → Visualization → Insights**

The analysis includes data preprocessing, univariate analysis, bivariate analysis, numerical analysis, and multivariate analysis.

---

## 📈 Key Insights

- **48.81%** of employees reported receiving mental health treatment.
- Employees with a **family history** reported a much higher treatment rate.
- Higher **work interference** was linked with higher treatment responses.
- Employees with **mental health benefits** showed higher treatment responses.
- Family history showed one of the strongest relationships with treatment among the selected variables.

The analysis shows that employee mental health treatment is related to several personal and workplace factors.

These insights can help organizations identify where **mental health awareness, support, and accessibility** can be improved.
